SUPER SIMPLE - ADD NEW IMAGES
=============================

STEP 1: Put your images here
   → Open the "new-images" folder
   → Copy your image files into it

STEP 2: Double-click this file
   → add-image.sh

That's it! Your images are added automatically.

EXAMPLE:
- You have: photo1.jpg, photo2.jpg
- Put them in "new-images" folder  
- Double-click "add-image.sh"
- Done! They become img14.jpg, img15.jpg in your site
